Integrations & Sync
App Icon
BA

Any image works — you'll crop it to a square next. Replaces the "BA" mark in the sidebar and the browser tab icon for everyone.

Your Name

Set once per person — auto-fills whenever you log an improvement, so you don't type it every time.

Approvers
Client Field Options

Add or remove the choices available in the Service and Status dropdowns when adding/editing clients. Importance (1–10) and Danger (1–5) are fixed numeric scales.

Custom Fields

Add your own fields to the client form — Website, Phone Number, anything you need. They'll show up when adding or editing a client, and on each client's detail page. Reorder with the arrows; deleting a field hides it going forward but keeps existing values.

Email Structure & Instructions

Tell the AI exactly how you want every client email structured — greeting style, paragraph order, sign-off, tone, length, anything. This applies to every draft it generates from here on. A few rules always apply no matter what you write here: every improvement must be described as a specific, measurable deliverable (never vague claims), and nothing that could make the agency look bad.

Google Sheet Sync

Live pull (Sheet → App): In your Google Sheet, go to File → Share → Publish to web, choose the specific tab, select CSV, and publish. Paste that link below. This reads the sheet — it can't write back to it.

Columns recognized (any order, case-insensitive): Name, Email, Service, Status, Importance, Danger, SOW.

If publishing to web isn't an option, paste the sheet's cells directly (select all in Sheets, copy, paste here):

To push changes back to the Sheet, export the current roster and paste it over your sheet's data.
